Data provenance & interpretation

This is a single-plate subset of the existing all-timepoint UMAP, not an embedding refit on the selected plate. Original UMAP coordinates were computed independently within each cancer group across nine plates. Distances across cancer-group UMAPs are not comparable.

The published sampling selected 500 cells per cell line per plate after QC. Only exact crop-key matches for this plate are displayed; no other-plate or background points are included.
